# Flight-Delay-ROI-Analysis
Business oriented analysis of airline delays quantifying cost, efficiency, and ROI opportunities with visual insights. This project analyzesairline delay data from a business and financial perspective. It quantifies total delay minutes, estimates the financial impact of those delays, and simulates potential ROI from improving operational efficiency.
Using Python (pandas, matplotlib), the project demonstrates how aviation data can be ransformed into actionable insights showing airline and regulators wher delay reduction delivers the highest returns.
# Insights
This project demonstrates how aviation data can be trned into financial intelligence. By quantifying inefficiencies. it eables:

Cost-based prioritization of delay reduction strategies.

Smart budget allocation.

Evidence-backed decision making at both airline and policy levels.

High Cost Airlines - A few airlines for the majority of total delay cost.

Controllable Factors Matter Most - Carrier and late aircraft delays represent most of the cost and are directly manageable.

ROI Clarity - Some airlines yield exceptionally high ROI for modest improvements.

10% Efficiency Gain - Even small operational improvement can save millions annually.
